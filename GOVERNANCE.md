# JAHID.AI Governance v27.2

Human authority controls ownership, legal registrations, credentials, production access, financial controls, destructive operations, security response and governance policy.

Agents may propose, prepare, test, analyze and execute authorized operations within their assigned permissions.

High-risk actions require the approval controls defined by the applicable policy.

All changes must remain traceable through version control, review, provenance and release records where technically supported.

The v27.2 governance layer governs the v25.x platform foundation and v26.0 Unified Data & Memory Fabric; it does not replace those systems.
